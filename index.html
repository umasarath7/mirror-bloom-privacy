Privacy Policy

Mirror Bloom: Logic Puzzle · by Mirror Bloom Games

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Mirror Bloom: Logic Puzzle ("the app", "we") uses, and why. The app is published by Mirror Bloom Games.

The short version

  • We do not run any servers, and we do not ask you to create an account.
  • Your progress, stars, painted patterns and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you buy Remove ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet so the game remembers where you are: finished levels and stars, daily and weekly puzzle results and streaks, badges, unfinished boards, Zen puzzles, the colours you painted, and your settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion, daily reminder). This information is not sent to us. If you have Android's built-in backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this information from the device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about your device and the app (for example model, operating system, language, app version), ad interactions, and diagnostic data.

Ads may be personalised where the law allows and you have agreed. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the app shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only records on your device whether Remove ads was bought, and asks Google Play to restore it if you reinstall.

4. Sharing and saving pictures

If you tap Share or Save as wallpaper, the app creates a picture or a short text about your puzzle on your device and passes it to the app you choose, or saves it to your photos. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

5. Daily reminder

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. Turn it off at any time in Settings → Daily reminder.

6. Feedback emails

Settings → Send feedback opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. It adds the app version, your phone model and Android version, and your star count, so we can find problems. You see the whole email and decide whether to send it. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, use them only to reply and to improve the game, and delete them when they're no longer needed. Ask us to delete them any time.

7. Children

Mirror Bloom is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side, apart from any feedback emails you chose to send us (see section 6). For information collected by Google for advertising, you can use the device and Google settings described above, or contact Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
